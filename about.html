Benjamin Rostance (He/Him/They/Them) is a neurodivergent, working class artist from the East Midlands whose practice is trauma informed and shaped by lived experience, recovery, and the complexities of privilege, disadvantage, and intersectional identity. Rostance’s work explores mental health, trauma, and the emotional realities of growing up in economically disadvantaged communities, and how those experiences continue to echo into adulthood.

Rostance’s visual language is rooted in working class environments, drawing on the textures, materials, and cultural markers of council estate life. Their practice is trauma informed, politically aware, and grounded in working class experience. Rostance’s work challenges assumptions about class, masculinity, and mental health, honouring the diversity and nuance within communities that are often misrepresented or overlooked. Rostance is a proud member of the New Contemporaries 2021 cohort.

Benjamin graduated from Nottingham Trent University with an exceptional first class degree in Contemporary Fine Art and later completed a master’s degree at the University of Derby, becoming an HCPC registered Art Psychotherapist in 2026. They have worked with institutions including the National Trust, University of Nottingham, Nottingham Contemporary, and continues to exhibit and sell work nationally and internationally.
