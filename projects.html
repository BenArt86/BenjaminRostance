I am a neurodivergent, queer artist from the East Midlands whose practice is shaped by lived experience, recovery, and the complexities of privilege, disadvantage, and identity.

My work explores mental health, trauma, and the emotional realities of growing up in economically disadvantaged communities — and how those experiences continue to echo into adulthood.

My practice is trauma-informed, politically aware, and grounded in working-class experience.

Current collaborations include work with Dr. K. Langsdale and General Practice Lincoln on a series of exhibitions for 2026.

This page will expand as new projects develop — including exhibitions, digital archives, workshops, and community-led initiatives.
