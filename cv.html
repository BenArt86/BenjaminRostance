Artist Overview

Benjamin Rostance is a working‑class fine artist whose practice examines the emotional, political, and psychological realities of lived experience, with a particular focus on recovery, identity, and working‑class narratives. Since 2017, he has developed a multidisciplinary body of work spanning drawing, performance, installation, and socially engaged projects. His work has been exhibited at South London Gallery, Firstsite Colchester, National Trust England, Nottingham Contemporary, New Art Exchange, General Practice Gallery, and Air Gallery, reflecting a practice grounded in trauma‑informed methodologies, accessibility, and community collaboration.
