My practice spans independent studio work, national and international exhibitions, collaborative projects across film and gaming, digital archiving, and accessibility work with arts organisations.

I have contributed to BAFTA-nominated film work, archived fine art VHS collections for universities and charities, and supported adults with learning differences for over 20 years.

I work at the intersection of art, care, and community — creating spaces where people can engage with creativity in ways that feel safe, meaningful, and accessible.
